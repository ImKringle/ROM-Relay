Before you start

What happens?

  1. Your base ROM is copied into private staging.
  2. The patch runs against that copy, never your source.
  3. The output is validated and gets a green Patched badge.
  4. The result expires after 24 hours or its latest completed download.

Only the private admin workspace can make a result permanent or approve a checksum mismatch.

Supported: IPS, UPS, BPS, PPF, RUP, APS, BSDiff, PMSR, and VCDiff.
