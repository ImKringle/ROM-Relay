Before you start

What happens?

  1. Your base ROM is copied into private staging.
  2. The randomizer runs against that copy, never your source.
  3. The output is validated and added to your library.
  4. The result expires after 24 hours unless you check "keep longer".

Supported: Game Boy, Game Boy Color, Game Boy Advance, Nintendo DS (Gens 1-5). 3DS games are not supported yet.
